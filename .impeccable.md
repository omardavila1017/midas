## Design Context

### Users
Finance and treasury professionals at Grupo Senda (large Mexican corporate group). Primary roles: CFO, tesorero, analistas financieros, controllers. Daily heavy use during business hours (8am-7pm). Power users — they live in this tool. They come from JDE Cognos / Excel and expect dense, fast, keyboard-friendly UIs. Spanish (es-MX) speakers. Currency context is always MXN. They need to project cash flow, reconcile bank movements, manage CXP, build scenarios/propuestas. Decisions move millions of pesos — trust matters.

### Brand Personality
Three words: **institucional, preciso, confiable** (institutional, precise, trustworthy).
- Voice: claro, directo, sin adornos. Reads like a financial statement, not a marketing site.
- Emotional goal: confidence and control. Never anxiety, never playfulness.
- Mexican corporate gravitas. Conservative. Senda is family-owned, decades-old, takes itself seriously.

### Aesthetic Direction
- Refined editorial-financial. Think Bloomberg Terminal meets modern data tooling (Stripe Sigma, Linear, Pylon). Dense but breathable.
- **Theme**: Light mode primary (office context, daylight hours, finance convention). Dark mode supported for evening close-out work.
- Already uses OKLCH design tokens via Senda DS (see `src/index.css`).
- Anti-references: no fintech-bro neon, no glassmorphism dashboards, no gradient cards, no AI slop. No marketing-site flourishes.
- Reference vibes: classical accounting paper grids, mexican modernist signage (Lance Wyman, '68 olympics), institutional banking (Banxico style).

### Design Principles
1. **Density over decoration.** Treasurers want to see more on one screen, not fewer. Whitespace is rhythm, not padding theater.
2. **Numbers are sacred.** Tabular figures, right-aligned MXN, monospace where appropriate, never break formatting. `formatters.ts` is the single chokepoint.
3. **Keyboard-first.** Command palette, shortcuts, no required mouse. Power users will never click what they can type.
4. **No drama in UI states.** Loading, errors, empty — informative and quiet. No bouncing spinners, no celebratory animations. Animations only for state transitions that need to be perceived (panel open, row reveal, focus change).
5. **Trust signals everywhere.** Show data provenance (JDE timestamp, last sync, "real" vs "proyectado"). Treasurers verify before they act.
